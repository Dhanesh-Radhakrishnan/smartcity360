SmartCity360

City operations console. Each module below applies one design pattern to one city problem.

Modules

  1. A Data stream security Encryption, compression and authentication layers on energy meter streams. Decorator
  2. B Smart zones Report usage or shut down power at any level, from city to building. Composite
  3. C Citizen complaints Escalation from local staff to supervisor to city manager, by severity. Chain of Responsibility
  4. D Smart street lights Tens of thousands of lights sharing one copy of their type data. Flyweight
  5. E City policies Noise and parking rules written as text, parsed and checked live. Interpreter
